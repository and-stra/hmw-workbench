# M9 - Player models

Status: **done with a documented asset gap** (2 of the 3 requested factions render; the missing
character assets are listed). Commit: `M9: player models`.

## 1. Research: how HMW builds a character

- The HMW costume script is `refs\dumps\hmw_files\scripts\mp_patches\custom_teams.gsc`. It
  `replacefunc`s `_teams::playercostume` / `setteammodels` and precaches a body, a head and a
  viewhands per faction, e.g.
  `tf141_assault_precache()`: `body_h2_tf141_assault`, `head_h2_tf141_assault`,
  `viewhands_tf141`; `opfor_assault`: `body_h2_opforce_assault` + `head_h2_opforce_assault`;
  `spetsnaz_assault`: `body_h2_airborne_assault` + `head_h2_airborne_assault`; `rangers_assault`:
  `body_h2_us_army_ar` + `head_h2_us_army_assault`; `seals_assault`: `body_h2_seal_assault` +
  `head_h2_seal_assault`; `militia_assault`: `body_h2_militia_assault` +
  `head_h2_militia_assault`; plus GIGN/PMC/Inner Circle/African Militia/Delta and the
  `*_smg`/`*_lmg` variants.
- H1's costume tables (`refs\h1_dump\h1_dump\mp\costumeidtable.csv`, `costumemodeltable.csv`,
  `agentcostumetable.csv`, `playercostume` in `refs\iw5-scripts\maps\mp\gametypes\_teams.gsc`)
  use the same body+head split; HMW ports them to the H2 bodies in custom_teams.gsc.
- Third-person player anims exist in the dumps as `mp_*` / `pb_*` xanims: `mp_jug_maniac_idle`
  (195 frames, 93 bones), `mp_jug_maniac_aim_*`, `pb_jug_maniac_*`, `mp_mantle_*` etc. Their bone
  names match the 102-bone player body (`j_mainroot`, `j_spine*`, `j_arm*`, `j_wrist_*`), so the
  workbench applies them to the body by bone name (`scene::BuildModelPose`).
- **Hand tag, verified from `-info body_hazmat`:** `tag_weapon_right` is parented to
  `j_wrist_ri` (the right hand/wrist) at world `(9.965, -29.093, 39.142)`; `tag_inhand` is
  parented to `j_spine4` (the chest) at `(10.492, -28.520, 38.933)`. The workbench mounts the
  world weapon on **`tag_weapon_right`** (fallback `tag_inhand`, then `tag_weapon`) and reports
  the tag/world position.
- **Asset gap:** the current roots contain only three player bodies -
  `body_hazmat` (102 bones, body includes the head), `body_infect` (103 bones, includes the
  head) and `body_usmc_desert_assault_mp_camo` (xmodel present, but its surface set
  `usmc_desert_assault_body_mp_lod0300.xsb` is **not** in any root). None of the HMW
  `body_h2_*`/`head_h2_*`/`mp_body_*`/`head_*` models are in the dumps (they are in HMW `.ff`
  zones that were not dumped; this run may not write outside the repo). The full table is in
  `review\M9\missing-factions.txt`.

## 2. Implementation

- `src\assets\character.h/.cpp` (new): the faction table from custom_teams.gsc (id, display,
  body, head, viewhands), availability check (`bodyFound`, `headFound`, `viewhandsFound`) and
  `LoadCharacter` (body + optional head, missing head only warns).
- `src\app\commands.*`: `LoadCharacter(faction, weapon)`, `LoadCharacterParts(body, head, weapon)`,
  `SetCharacterWeapon`, `ClearCharacter`, character state/palettes/warnings/missing list.
  The body is posed with `BuildModelPose`; a separate head xmodel is mapped by bone name and
  attached at `j_head`/`tag_head`/`j_neck`/`tag_neck` (the dumps have no head models, see gap);
  the world weapon root is attached at `tag_weapon_right`/`tag_inhand`/`tag_weapon`.
- UI Inspector → **Character**: faction dropdown with body/head availability, body/head/weapon
  status, missing-asset warnings, weapon input + Mount, IDLE/AIM/HOLD third-person anim buttons,
  Clear.
- `-character <faction> -character-weapon <weapon>` (plus `-anim`/`-time`/`-view`/`-screenshot`).
- MCP `load_character {faction | body+head, weapon, anim, time}`; `get_bones`/`get_tags` and
  `screenshot` now accept a loaded character (`source: "character"`).

## 6. Open issues / deviations

- **Only 2 complete factions are in the dumps.** The HMW `body_h2_*`/`head_h2_*` models and the
  H1 `mp_body_*`/`head_*` models are missing; dumping them would write a new folder under
  `GAME\dump\`, which this run's rules forbid. `usmc_desert` is blocked on its missing xsurface.
  Once those dumps exist, the faction table is ready (no code change needed for the listed ids).
- Head attachment is implemented (`j_head`/`tag_head` mapping) but cannot be tested because no
  separate head xmodels are in the dumps; the two available bodies include their heads.
- The third-person clips in the dumps are the juggernaut (`mp_jug_maniac_*`/`pb_jug_maniac_*`)
  set; faction rifle idle/hold clips were not dumped. The anim application is generic, so any
  dumped player anim will work.
- The character camera is framed from the bind-pose bounds; an anim that moves the body far from
  the bind bounds can leave it partly outside a fixed `-dist`; use `-dist`/`-yaw` to reframe.
- No writes outside the repo in this milestone.
