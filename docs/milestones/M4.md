# M4 - Attachments (+ crossbow diagnosis)

Status: **done**. Commit: `M4: attachments`.

## Goal

1. Diagnose the open in-game bug "crossbow very close / partially clipped at the correct FOV"
   with numbers, and say what would fix it in the port (report only, no port files changed).
2. Implement HMW attachments: verify the mounting rule, load every `attachments/*.json`, mount
   view + reticle models, apply `hideTags`/`showTags`, the weapon `animOverrides` and the
   attachment `fields` anim suffixes, add the Inspector picker, a plain-data fit report, and the
   CLI `-attach` / `-fit-report`.

the weapon (`j_wrist_le` at the rail, `j_wrist_ri` at the trigger).

### What H1 does with the animated tags and the view offsets

Verified in the dumps, the read-only H1 structs (`cod-mwx/src/client/game/database.hpp`), the
H1/IW5 GSC dumps and x64-zt source (`.ref/x64-zt`):

- The viewmodel is a DObj of hands + weapon (+ attachments). Animations are applied **by bone
  name, parent-relative**. `tag_torso` is the arms root, `tag_ads` is the ADS pivot (every
  `ADS_UP`/`ADS_DOWN` anim in the dumps only animates `tag_ads`), `tag_camera` is the camera
  reference bone, `tag_player` links the third-person player body
  (`playerlinkto* ( ..., "tag_player" )` in the IW5 scripts). `tag_player` is not part of the
  gun chain, so animating it does not move the weapon.
- H1's `tag_weapon_right` / `tag_knife_attach` are aliases for `tag_weapon`
  (`FUN_16013d1bc` in `refs/hmw_decomp/decomp_001.c`, and the same hook in
  `cod-mwx/src/client/component/weapon.cpp`). The crossbow has no such aliases; its root is
  `j_gun` under the hands' `tag_weapon`.
- The weapon JSON `standMove`/`standRot`/`strafeMove`/`strafeRot`/`duckedOfs` are world /
  third-person view offsets, not viewmodel offsets. x64-zt reads/writes them as plain
  `WeaponDef` fields (`weapondef.cpp`, offsets in `database.hpp`: standMove at 1416, duckedOfs
  at 1464). **Both weapons have all zero** (crossbow also has `duckedOfs = (0,0,-1)`), so they
  cannot explain a difference. There is no separate viewmodel offset field in the weapon JSON.
- The HMW hands have `tag_view` = `tag_camera` = (0,0,60), and the crossbow idle animates
  `tag_camera` rotation only (identity). Using `tag_view` as the camera is therefore equivalent
  for this pose. `tag_ads` is applied by the workbench as the end pose of `ADS_UP` (M4 verified
  against the reference); the crossbow's `ADS_UP` moves `tag_ads` by (-3.430, 3.161, 1.260) and
  puts its sight at screen (960, 686) - horizontally centered, 178 px below the crosshair.

### Conclusion

- **No bone rename, root offset, anim channel or weapon-JSON offset explains an oversized or
  closer crossbow.** The camera, `tag_view`/`tag_camera`, `tag_ads`, `tag_torso`'s parent chain,
  `tag_player` and all view offsets are identical to the M4; the optic distance is 12.17 vs
  12.23 units and the wrists project within 15 px of the M4's. The only real differences are the
  AW hip pose (gun root 1.73 units lower) and the model itself.
- The "oversized / partially clipped" look is the **crossbow model**: 21.97 units wide vs 2.90
  for the M4, and a built-in sight assembly - surfaces 0, 1, 3, 18, 19 (`mtl_crossbow_scope_base`,
  `ms/mtl_crossbow_scope_base`, `mtl_crossbow_target`, `mtl_crossbow_glass_base`,
  `mtl_iron_sights_color`, 1844 verts) are 100% rigid to `tag_sight_on` at 15.1 units. Those
  surfaces are the big ring + glass in the screenshots; their materials are also missing from
  the dump (checker). The stock's rear (-3.0 vs -2.3) is the clipped part.
- What would fix it in the port (one of these, not applied here):
  1. Hide the `tag_sight_on` assembly when an optic is mounted, or hide the scope-base/glass
     surfaces on the bare crossbow. This is a `hideTags`/port-data change (the AW attachment
     hide tags were not ported; the crossbow's `hideTags` is empty and HMW optics' `hideTags`
     are empty too). With those five surfaces hidden the bulky ring disappears (`-hidesurface
     0,1,3,18,19`, see the M4 hideTags support).
  2. If the desired look is the M4-like framing, move the whole viewmodel root up by ~1.7 and
     forward by ~2.3 units (or scale the viewmodel ~0.85) - a pure workaround; it also moves the
     hands and is not justified by the rig.
  3. Do not drop the `tag_torso`/`tag_weapon` anim channels: that would centre the crossbow
     horizontally (y=0) and break the AW hip pose; the M4 proves the channels are applied
     correctly.
- **No port files were changed.**

The crossbow screenshots (`crossbow_hip.png`, `crossbow_ads.png`, `crossbow_vs_m4_hip.png`) show
this: side by side at the same settings the crossbow is lower and smaller on screen, with the
wide limbs and the large ring sight; it is not closer than the M4.

## Attachments

### Mounting rule (verified)

Attachment models place themselves by their **first bone name**, which is the weapon tag they
mount on. Verified for the assets and the engine path:

| attachment view model | root bone |
|---|---|
| `attach_h1_optic_reflex_vm` | `tag_reflex` |
| `attach_h1_scope_acog_vm` | `tag_acog` |
| `attach_h1_scope_thermal_vm_mp` | `tag_thermal_scope` |
| `attach_h1_silencer_556_vm` | `tag_silencer` |
| `attach_jup_silencer_lmg` | `tag_silencer1` |
| `reticle_h1_optic_reflex` / `reticle_h1_reflex_transparent` | `tag_reticle_attach` |

- `Refs/iw5-scripts` and the HMW GSC dumps mount by `(model, tag)`:
  `self attach( getattachmodelname(i), getattachtagname(i) )` (`_weapons.gsc`), and
  `getattachtagname` is the attachment's mount tag. The H1 alias function `FUN_16013d1bc`
  resolves `tag_weapon_right`/`tag_knife_attach` to `tag_weapon` by bone name.
- x64-zt's `WeaponAttachment` has `viewModels`, `reticleViewModels`, `hideTags`, `showTags` and
  `waFields` - **no position/rotation fields**, so the root bone is the only placement data.
- Reticles mount on the attachment's `tag_reticle_attach` (the reflex attachment's is at
  (0.591, 0, 1.264); both reflex reticle models have their root at the origin). The workbench
  maps the reticle root to that tag.

### What was implemented

- `src/assets/attachment.{h,cpp}`: reads `attachments/*.json` from every root (104 assets; 74
  are the HMW set), with view/world/reticle models, hide/show tags and `fields`; nearest-tag
  scoring and the AW optic index map.
- `Workbench::Attach/Detach/DetachAll/Attachments`: loads the view model + all reticle models,
  uploads them, resolves the required tag, and mounts:
  `mount = inverse(viewmodel root bind) * pose(tag)` for found tags;
  missing tags are drawn at the weapon origin (`j_gun`) in red, on top
  (`weapon has no tag_reflex`, nearest candidates in the Inspector and fit report).
- `hideTags`/`showTags`: weapon + active attachment `hideTags`, minus `showTags`; hidden bones get
  a zero palette matrix (their rigid vertices collapse), attachments mounted on a hidden tag are
  skipped. This is the tag-hide mechanism; it is approximate for blended vertices.
- `animOverrides`: an override applies when its `attachment1` matches an active attachment
  (index into the weapon's attachment list for H1 weapons, AW optic family map for the crossbow)
  and its `animTreeType` maps to the requested weapon state. `128`/`129` are treated as
  `ADS_UP`/`ADS_DOWN` (AW enum; H1 uses 129/130) because the crossbow's overrides are ADS anims.
- Attachment `fields`: `szXAnims ADS_UP "_ads _reflex_ads"` replaces the first token in the
  state's anim name and uses the result if that `.xab` exists. H1 weapons have e.g.
  `h1_wpn_asl_xmlar_reflex_ads_up`; the H2 M4 has no `vm_h2_m4a1_reflex_ads_up`, so it falls
  back to `vm_h2_m4a1_ads_up` (reported in the UI/effective dump).
- Inspector: attachment search + multi-select checkboxes; per entry the view model, needed tag,
  found/MISSING (red) and the nearest 3 weapon tags; "Detach all"; hidden-tag line; a red
  "weapon has no ..." hint. The animOverrides list now shows `tree N -> STATE [active]`.
- Command layer: the above commands plus `BuildFitReport`/`PrintFitReport` returning plain data
  (`AttachmentFitReport`, `AttachmentFitEntry`) for the M4b MCP; no ImGui or loader calls above
  it.
- CLI: repeatable `-attach <name>`, `-fit-report [file]`, plus review helpers `-posedump [file]`,
  `-crosshair`, `-marktags`, `-caption <text>`, `-hidesurface <i,...>`.

### `animOverrides[].attachment1`

It is an index into **`WeaponDef::attachments`** (`WeaponAttachment**`, x64-zt
`weapondef.cpp`: `weapon->attachments[i]`, and `animOverrides[i].attachment1` is read from the
JSON at the same index). The crossbow's `attachments` list is **empty** while its 21 overrides
use 1..7, i.e. the AW indices were not carried over by the port. The override anim names give the
mapping, which the workbench uses for playback:

| index | AW optic | override anims seen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ACOG | `vm_crossbow_acog_ads_up/down` |
| 2 | EOTech/holo | `vm_crossbow_eotech_ads_up/down` |
| 3 | red dot | `vm_crossbow_reddot_ads_up/down` |
| 4 | thermal | `vm_crossbow_thermal_ads_up/down`, `vm_global_thermal_open_additive` |
| 5 | VRD | `vm_crossbow_vred_ads_up/down`, `vm_crossbow_vred_up/down`, `vm_crossbow_vred_additive_state` |
| 6 | target enhancer | `vm_crossbow_enhancer_ads_up/down` |
| 7 | scope | `vm_crossbow_scope_ads_up/down`, `vm_crossbow_fire_ads`, `vm_crossbow_fire_last_ads` |

(`animTreeType`: 128/129 = ADS up/down, 47/48 = hybrid sight on/off, 64/65 = ADS fire/lastshot,
2 = empty idle, 133/135 = additive ADS/hybrid roots.) Because the list is empty, H1's own
index lookup cannot work; the port needs the AW attachment list (or a family map) for these
overrides to apply in game.

## Self-check: H2 M4 reflex ADS

```
hmw-workbench.exe -screenshot review\M4\m4_reflex_ads_end.png -weapon h2_m4_mp -view firstperson
                  -ads -anim vm_h2_m4a1_idle -time 0 -attach reflexmwrm4 -crosshair
```

The reflex reticle centre is at **exactly (960, 508)**, the image/crosshair centre (the dot sits
on the crosshair in `m4_reflex_ads_end.png`). The workbench gets there by aligning `tag_ads` to
the mounted reticle in ADS: after the ADS overlay it moves `tag_ads` by the residual between the
reticle aim point and the camera axis (clamped to 5 units). The game does this with the
attachment-specific `_reflex_ads` anim; that `.xab` is not in the H2 M4 dumps, so the alignment is
an approximation (documented as an open issue). M4 default iron sights (no attachment) still use
the plain ADS overlay from M3.1 and are ~13 px off centre, as before.

## Open issues

- Crossbow model/sight (see the Crossbow conclusion): the built-in `tag_sight_on` assembly and
  the wide limbs are the visible "oversized" part; the five surfaces' materials are missing from
  the dump (checker). Port-level fix not applied.
- The H2 M4's own reflex attachment JSON is not in the dumps; the review uses the HMW
  `reflexmwrm4` (H1 reflex view model). It mounts on `tag_reflex`; the H1 reflex body is visually
  large. The H2 `attach_h2_reflex_vm` model exists but has no attachment JSON.
- The game's attachment-specific ADS anims (`_reflex_ads`, `_acog_ads`) are missing for the H2
  M4, so ADS is a `tag_ads` end-pose plus the reticle alignment described above. No ADS
  transition timing/sway.
- `hideTags` is approximated with a zero palette matrix (rigid vertices collapse; blended
  vertices get a partial collapse). No per-surface culling.
- Reticle models are drawn opaque, in order; the transparent lens (`reticle_h1_reflex_transparent`)
  is drawn as well. There is no alpha blending, so the lens can hide the dot from some angles.
- `-fit-report` scans all 104 attachment assets across the roots (including non-HMW entries with
  no view model) and does not filter weapon compatibility. The crossbow report shows the 74 HMW
  attachments as MISSING except the thermal (`tag_thermal_scope` exists) and AW tags that happen
  to match; `tag_reflex` -> `tag_red_dot, tag_eotech`.
- The crossbow's override `attachment1` indices cannot be resolved through
  `WeaponDef::attachments` (empty); the workbench uses the verified family map above. The port
  should restore the list.
- `FUN_16013d1bc` / `xmodel_get_bone_index` is the only tag-resolution function recovered from
  the stripped H1 decomp; the exact mount call could not be recovered, so the rule was verified
  from the H1/IW5 GSC `attach(model, tag)` path, x64-zt's struct/serializer and the
  root-bone names of all attachment view models.
